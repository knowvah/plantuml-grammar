## Observation: the pin lives in two places
- **Context**: Writing "Regenerating after an upstream bump".
- **Finding**: `upstream-pin.json` (extractor, oracle cache key) and `PIN_SHA` in `tools/config.py`
  (default jar name, provenance reader) must be changed together.
- **Impact**: Documented in the guide; a bump that changes only one gives a mixed pin.
- **Confidence**: High.
